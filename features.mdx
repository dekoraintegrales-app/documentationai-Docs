## Core Offerings

### Custom Kitchens

Custom kitchens are often the central offer in a furniture catalog. You can describe them as functional, durable, and tailored to cooking habits, storage needs, and available space. A strong kitchen section usually includes layout options, surface materials, and finish combinations.

### Closets and Vestiers

Closets and vestiers help users organize clothing, shoes, and personal items with better visibility and access. When you document this category, focus on internal divisions, hanging zones, drawers, shelves, and mirrored or open-front variants.

### Bathroom Furniture

Bathroom furniture should feel compact, resistant, and easy to maintain. In your documentation, explain how vanities, storage cabinets, and mirror units support small or medium spaces while keeping the area tidy.

### Entertainment Centers

Entertainment centers work best when you describe them as adaptable pieces for televisions, devices, and decorative objects. Mention cable management, shelving, closed storage, and visual balance so users understand the value of a custom build.

### Workstations and Accessories

Workstations are useful for home offices, study corners, and hybrid work setups. Accessories can include auxiliary shelves, side tables, and support modules that complete the space without overwhelming it.
