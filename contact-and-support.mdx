## Overview

Use this page as a starter kit for your contact and support process. You can adapt the channels, response times, and booking flow to match your team’s actual operating hours and service model.

For Diseño Dekora, the goal is simple: help people reach the right channel quickly, prepare the right project details, and move from first contact to a scheduled visit without friction. The sections below give you a reusable structure for that workflow.

<Callout kind="info">

This is sample documentation for a starter kit. Replace the examples with your own phone numbers, forms, social profiles, and service policies.

</Callout>

## Booking an in-person visit

Use a simple booking flow so clients know what to expect before they arrive. This is especially helpful when you need to review measurements, materials, and budget in person.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit your request" icon="send">
    
    Ask the client to share the project type, preferred date, and contact details.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm availability" icon="calendar">
    
    Check your calendar and propose one or two visit windows.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the location and instructions" icon="map-pin">
    
    Send the address, parking guidance, and any access details needed for the visit.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the meeting" icon="clipboard-check">
    
    Review the project summary before the appointment so you can give focused guidance.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">

If you expect a high volume of visits, add a booking link and a short intake form to collect details before the meeting.

</Callout>

## Required project details

Request the same core information from every lead. That makes it easier for your team to estimate effort, plan the visit, and respond consistently.

<ParamField body="projectType" param-type="string" required="true">
  Describe the space or product you want to design, such as kitchen, closet, or bathroom furniture.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="location" param-type="string" required="true">
  Share the city or neighborhood so the team can confirm travel and service coverage.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="approximateBudget" param-type="string" required="false">
  Provide a budget range to help the team suggest realistic options.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="preferredTimeline" param-type="string" required="false">
  Tell the team when you would like the project to start or be completed.
</ParamField>

### Example intake payload

```json
{
  "name": "Laura Pérez",
  "projectType": "Kitchen renovation",
  "location": "Medellín, Antioquia",
  "approximateBudget": "COP 18,000,000 to COP 25,000,000",
  "preferredTimeline": "Within the next 6 weeks",
  "contactMethod": "WhatsApp"
}
```

## Response expectations

Set expectations early so clients understand when and how they will hear back. A transparent process improves trust and reduces duplicate messages.

  <Tab title="Visit scheduling" icon="calendar">

## Social media and follow-up

Use social channels to reinforce your brand voice and keep the conversation going after the first contact. You can share project highlights, behind-the-scenes updates, and reminders to book a visit.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What should I send after the first reply?" default-open="true">

    Send a short confirmation, restate the requested service, and list the next step.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I handle missed appointments?" default-open="false">

    Offer a polite reschedule option and ask the client to confirm a new time.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What if I need more project details?" default-open="false">

    Request measurements, reference images, and any material preferences before quoting.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
